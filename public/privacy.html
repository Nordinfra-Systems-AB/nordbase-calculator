Privacy Policy

NordBase Foundation Selector — last updated September 5, 2026
This page describes what the NordBase Foundation Selector calculator (calculator.nordbaseusa.com) actually does with the information you submit. It is a plain description of our practices, not a substitute for legal advice — if you have questions about how a specific privacy law applies to your situation, please consult your own advisor.

What we collect

When you use the calculator, you may enter:

  • Contact details you provide voluntarily — your name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project information — a project name, project address/state, and the number of foundations.
  • Your calculation inputs and results — the foundation, charger, site (wind speed, seismic value, backfill), and package selections you make, and the resulting sizing/compliance output.

Nothing here is required to use the calculator or generate a report. Contact and project fields are only used on the report cover sheet, and are only sent anywhere if you click the download button described below.

How we use it

If you check the consent box and click "Download … package," we collect and use the information you submit (including your contact details and your calculation) to email you your results and follow up about your project, and, where applicable, to share it with an authorized Nordinfra distribution partner in your region to help process an order. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Where it's stored

When you submit a calculation, it is sent to two places at once:

  • An email is sent to Nordinfra via our email provider, Resend.
  • A record is created in our internal project/lead-tracking system (ClickUp) so our team can follow up.

We keep this information for as long as it's reasonably needed for the purposes above — generally, for the life of the sales relationship or project. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see "Your choices" below).

Cookies & local storage

The calculator saves your in-progress configuration in your browser's local storage so you don't lose your work if you close the tab, and so a "resume link" you copy and share can restore it. This data stays on your device and in the link itself — it is not sent to us unless you click download/send. We also store your cookie-banner choice locally so we don't ask again.

We do not currently use any third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we'll update this page and the cookie banner to reflect it.

Your choices

  • You can use and complete the entire calculator without providing any contact information — it's only needed if you want us to follow up.
  • You can decline non-essential cookies at any time via the cookie banner.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you've submitted, by contacting us below.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your information, can be sent to info@nord-infra.com.
